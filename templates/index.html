GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can I download any video from any platform?

No. Only sources enabled by the site operator are supported. You must own the content or have permission to download and convert it. Platform terms and copyright laws apply.

Will every video have 4K quality?

No. The available resolutions depend on the original source. NovaShift cannot create a higher resolution than the source provides.

Is NovaShift free?

This installation does not currently charge users. The site owner may add optional paid features later, provided they are clearly described and lawful.

How does the website earn money?

The owner may apply to an advertising network after publishing useful original content and meeting its policies. Approval and earnings are not guaranteed.
